Ingestion pipeline

XRPL sampling → entity classification → daily rollup

A Supabase edge function samples ~40 validated ledgers every 10 minutes (~25% daily coverage), classifies every successful XRP payment against XRPSCAN's registry of 2,772 known wallets, and rolls up daily metrics. Adjusted volume excludes same-entity transfers and Ripple treasury moves. Daily totals are extrapolated from coverage and labeled as such — swap in QuickNode Streams for 100% coverage without changing this dashboard.

    Data sources & quality

    Every figure is labeled with where it came from

    • Live
      CoinGecko API — price, market cap, volume, 12-month history, RLUSD supply. Free tier, no key.
    • Live
      XRPL Cluster (xrplcluster.com) — validated ledgers with expanded transactions; payment volume computed from delivered_amount metadata, successful non-self payments only.
    • Live
      s1.ripple.com (WebSocket) — RLUSD issuer obligations via gateway_balances.
    • Live
      Google News RSS — XRP/RLUSD headlines fetched by scripts/refresh_data.py, relevance-filtered, and re-filtered to a 72-hour window at view time.
    • Estimate
      Curated baseline (data/dashboard.json) — monthly volume series. Replace with the QuickNode → Supabase pipeline output when it's running; the file format is already pipeline-compatible.

    Adjusted volume excludes exchange-internal, treasury and self transfers per the classification methodology. Estimates are shown as estimates — one precise-looking number would overstate the analysis.
