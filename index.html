    Supply & DeFi Live

    Two supply-side forces: scheduled unlocks and on-ledger liquidity

    Escrow: Ripple locked most of its XRP in on-ledger escrows that release on a fixed schedule (~1B per month). Unlocks are predictable new supply — markets often trade around unlock dates, and "released" does not mean "sold" (most is typically re-escrowed). AMM liquidity: XRP pooled in the ledger's native automated market makers — a measure of how much can be traded on-ledger without moving the price, and where RLUSD's DeFi footprint shows up.

    Amendments — voting now Live

    Validator support toward the 80% threshold · enabled 2 weeks after majority holds

    Amendments are XRPL protocol upgrades — new features like lending, vaults or batch transactions. Each needs 28 of the 35 trusted validators voting yes for two continuous weeks. Vote momentum (the "this wk" chips) shows which features are gaining support; a majority chip starts a two-week activation countdown.

    Validators Live

    Mainnet consensus network · XRPSCAN registry

    The validator network is XRPL's security and decentralization story. The UNL (Unique Node List) is the 35-validator set trusted by default — amendments need 80% of them. The version spread shows how fast operators upgrade, which gates when new amendments can even be voted on.
